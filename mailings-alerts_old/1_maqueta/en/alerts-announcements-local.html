AI15067 - Domestic interchange modifications in select Southern and Eastern Africa and Western and Central Africa countries
Impact type:
New/Updated Edit 		Jurisdiction:
Selected countries (ASIA & ME/​AFR) 		Customer:
Issuer
Visa lowered the shared liability limit for domestic mass transit transactions in Panama to USD 2 and updated the rules for clearing after declined authorizations. Acquirers must submit only eligible declines and include authorization code VFT000, while issuers and processors must recognize and process these transactions correctly under the shared liability clearing process for urban mobility transactions in VisaNet and Visa Rules.
February 1, 2027 Monitoring begins for groups 1 and 2.
March 1, 2027 Notifications begin for groups 1 and 2.
May 1, 2027 Comply-by date for group 1.
June 27, 2027 Assessment date for group 1.
July 1, 2027 Comply-by date for group 2.
August 29, 2027 Assessment date for group 2.
